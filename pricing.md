# Pricing

Supergood Solutions publishes its prices. Currency: USD. Last updated 2026-10-29.

| Offer | Price | Unit | What it covers |

| Fit check | Free | one-time | Free. About 2 minutes. Describe the work and get a written answer on fit. If it is a fit, Ryan follows up to schedule the paid Quick Scan. |

## How it fits together

1. **Fit check (free).** About 2 minutes at https://supergood.solutions/start/. You describe the work and get a written answer on fit.
2. **Quick Scan ($1,000).** Scheduled after a fit. Credited toward a build if you sign within 14 days.
3. **Build (from $4,000 per workflow).** Fixed quote after the Quick Scan. Half due at kickoff, half at launch.
4. **Run (from $900 per month).** Plus 10-20% of measured gain over an agreed baseline, capped at 1.5x the retainer. 3-month minimum.

## Tools and software

Clients pay for tools, APIs and seats on their own accounts. Supergood does not resell or mark up software.
