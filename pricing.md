## How it fits together

1. **Intro call (free).** 20 minutes at https://supergood.solutions/start/. A straight answer on whether AI helps.
2. **Sidekick Session ($500).** 90 minutes on video. One thing set up live, a written list of the next three, and a one-page summary the next business day. Credited in full to a Setup Sprint signed within 14 days.
3. **Setup Sprint (from $2,500).** Two or three workflows in two to three weeks, written how-tos, and a one-page AI policy.
4. **Sidekick Monthly (from $900 per month).** A 30-minute weekly check-in, a running list worked from the top down, and a Friday one-page report. 3-month minimum. Optional 10-20% of measured gain on workflows the client's systems already track, capped at 1.5x the retainer.
5. **Quick Scan ($1,000), for teams of 10 or more.** Credited toward a build if you sign within 14 days.
6. **Build (from $4,000 per workflow), for teams of 10 or more.** Fixed quote after the Quick Scan. Half due at kickoff, half at launch.

Clients pay for tools, APIs and seats on their own accounts. Supergood does not resell or mark up software.
