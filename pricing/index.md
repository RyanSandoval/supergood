## FAQ

### What does the Quick Scan cost?

The Quick Scan is $1,000. It is credited toward a build if you sign within 14 days.

### What does a build cost?

A build starts at $4,000 per workflow. You get a fixed quote after the Quick Scan. Half is due at kickoff and half at launch.

### What does the Sidekick Session include?

The Sidekick Session is $500, paid when booked. Before the call: a 5-question form and access to the one tool you will work in. On the call: 90 minutes on video, and one workflow set up in your accounts. After: a one-page summary the next business day with the next three jobs, each with effort and monthly tool cost, and one week of email follow-up. The $500 is credited in full to a Setup Sprint signed within 14 days.

### What does a Setup Sprint include?

A Setup Sprint starts at $2,500. It builds the next two or three workflows in two to three weeks, with written how-tos and a one-page AI policy.

### How does Sidekick Monthly work?

Sidekick Monthly starts at $900 per month. It includes a 30-minute weekly check-in, a running list worked from the top down, and a Friday one-page report. The minimum is 3 months. Where a workflow has a number your systems already track, an optional 10-20% of measured gain can apply, capped at 1.5x the retainer.

### How is gain measured?

During the Quick Scan, each workflow gets a baseline number from your own systems, such as CRM timestamps, ticket logs, or order data. You sign off on that number. The baseline is not an estimate. Each month it is measured the same way and converted to dollars at a rate agreed up front. A workflow with no system-measurable number is a flat fee instead of a share of gain.

### Who pays for the software and tools?

You pay for the software and tools on your own accounts, including APIs and seats. Supergood does not resell or mark up software.

### How long does a build take?

Build length is quoted after the Quick Scan.

### Are founding client spots available?

Founding client spots are limited. They are for a business willing to be a published case study. Ask during the intro call.
