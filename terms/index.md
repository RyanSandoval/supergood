# Terms

## The content on this site

The pages and blog posts on supergood.solutions are informational. They are not a contract, a quote, or an offer. Work starts only after Ryan replies to you.

## Starting a project

The fit check at [https://supergood.solutions/start/](https://supergood.solutions/start/) sends Ryan a note. Sending it does not create an engagement. The site has no accounts and no payment form. Paying for a Quick Scan or a build happens after Ryan replies, off this domain.
